# 🇮🇳 HERITAGE 360
### *“Experience. Learn. Preserve India’s Living Heritage.”*
**Smart India Hackathon (SIH) 2026**

---

## 🌟 Overview
**HERITAGE 360** is an educational, immersive, and highly interactive digital platform built for students, teachers, researchers, museums, cultural organizations, and heritage lovers worldwide. It combines the visual gravitas of **Google Arts & Culture** with an **interactive pedagogical hub** and a **living cultural museum**, uniting five millennia of civilizational wisdom with cutting-edge conversational AI, spatial 3D digital twins, geospatial GIS mapping, and gamified challenges.

---

## 🏛️ Platform Architecture & 9 Core Modules

### 1. Home Page
- **Hero Canvas**: Cinematic branding, tagline, quick call-to-actions (*Explore Heritage*, *Start Your Journey*).
- **Interactive India Map Radar**: Live coordinate nodes highlighting civilizational hubs (Kedarnath, Hampi, Konark, Majuli Island).
- **Civilizational Metrics Counter**: 5,000+ Years of History, 42+ UNESCO World Heritage Sites, 400+ GI Crafts, 360° Spatial Digital Reconstructions.
- **Featured Experience Cards**: Direct portals to all core modules.
- **Daily Cultural Spotlight**: Deep-dive on indigenous technologies like the *Living Root Bridges of Meghalaya*.

### 2. AI Culture Avatar
- **Holographic Digital Guide**: HTML5 Canvas rendering a real-time animated avatar with rotating golden mandala halo, glowing Third Eye / tilak, blinking eyes, and audio-reactive mouth waveforms.
- **3 Specialized Personas**:
  - **Acharya Vikram**: Temple architecture, Vastu Shastra, stone engineering, and metallurgy.
  - **Devi Meera**: Classical dance, Bharatanatyam mudras, Hindustani & Carnatic ragas.
  - **Guru Kabir**: Living folklore, sacred seasonal festivals, tribal arts, and culinary traditions.
- **Speech-to-Text (STT)**: Microphone button utilizing the browser's Web Speech Recognition API with pulsating microphone feedback.
- **Text-to-Speech (TTS)**: Realistic audio playback of cultural responses with animated avatar speech synchronization.
- **Multilingual Support**: English, Hindi (हिंदी), Tamil (தமிழ்), Telugu (తెలుగు), Bengali (বাংলা), Marathi (मराठी), Gujarati (ગુજરાતી), and Sanskrit (संस्कृतम्).
- **Suggested Question Prompts**: Instant answers for Bharatanatyam mudras, Pongal history, Brihadisvara Temple acoustics, Madhubani art, and Tabla tuning physics.

### 3. Time Travel India
- **Four Chronological Eras**:
  1. *Ancient India & Indus Valley* (c. 2600 BCE – 500 CE)
  2. *Medieval Bharat & Imperial Dynasties* (c. 600 CE – 1700 CE)
  3. *Colonial Period & Renaissance* (c. 1750 – 1947 CE)
  4. *Modern & Living Bharat* (1947 – Present Day)
- **Multi-Facet Deep Dives**: Historical Events, Monumental Architecture, Traditional Clothing, Indigenous Food, Fine Arts & Crafts, and Sacred Cultural Locations.
- **Interactive 3D / 360° Monument Inspection Canvas**:
  - Full 360° mouse/touch rotation and scroll zoom.
  - Switchable monuments: *Konark Sun Temple wheel*, *Hampi Stone Chariot*, *Mohenjo-daro Great Bath*, and *Brihadisvara Temple*.
  - **Interactive 3D Hotspots**: Clickable glowing nodes revealing structural secrets (e.g. interlocking mortarless granite, solar sundial spokes, bitumen seals).
  - **Day / Sunset / Night celestial lighting toggle**.
  - **Ambient Acoustic Soundscape**: Real-time Web Audio API synthesizer generating a meditative Tanpura harmonic drone and crystalline temple bells.

### 4. Living Heritage Map
- **Dual Map View**:
  - High-fidelity **Interactive SVG Regional Map** with responsive hover states and glowing regional nodes.
  - **Full Leaflet GIS Map** with dark-gold CartoDB tiles and custom coordinates for over 25 cultural landmarks.
- **Multidimensional Filters**:
  - State Filter: Dropdown covering all 28 states & union territories.
  - Regional Tabs: North, South, East, West, Central, North-East.
  - Cultural Categories: All, Festivals, Classical Dance, Traditional Music, Master Crafts, Culinary Heritage, Temples, Languages.
- **Heritage Information Drawer**: Slide-out dossier displaying community lineages, living rituals, GI tags, audio rhythms, and one-click bookmarking.

### 5. Heritage Lab (Citizen Archiving)
- **"Document Your Heritage" Guided Workflow**:
  - 5-step modal capturing local traditions, generational origins, and preservation urgency.
  - **Live Oral History Audio Recorder**: In-browser microphone recording with waveform visual feedback and playback.
  - Grants **+100 XP** and queues the submission into the National Verification Portal.
- **Community Submissions Showcase**: Public gallery of verified student field documentations from across India with like and upvote counters.
- **Verified Learning Kits**: Downloadable study guides for *Temple Geometry*, *Lost-Wax Bronze Metallurgy*, and *Ancient Stepwells*.

### 6. Learn by Doing (Gamified Hub)
- **Cultural Quiz Challenge**: 5-question dynamic quiz with 20s countdown timer, instant historical explanation, score multiplier, and celebratory confetti.
- **Match-the-Art-Form Challenge**: Interactive card pair matching game pairing folk arts with their origin states (e.g., Madhubani - Bihar, Kathakali - Kerala, Pashmina - Kashmir, Dokra - Bengal).
- **Identify the Monument Game**: Clue-based mystery quiz with silhouette prompts and architectural trivia.
- **Virtual Tabla Percussion Studio**: Interactive pads for authentic bols (*Dha, Dhin, Ta, Na, Ge*) powered by a custom Web Audio API synthesizer and a 16-beat Teentaal loop player.
- **Sacred 8-Fold Radial Rangoli Canvas**: HTML5 Canvas with real-time 8-fold radial symmetry, customizable color palette, brush controls, preset sacred mandalas, and PNG export.
- **Badges System**: 8 unlockable badges (*Temple Architect, Raga Maestro, Rangoli Artisan, Folklorist, Quiz Champion, Map Navigator, Heritage Guardian, Avatar Scholar*).
- **National Leaderboards**: Real-time student rankings across All-India, Inter-College, and Inter-School categories.

### 7. Heritage Discovery
- Searchable catalog of 30+ comprehensive entries across 10 cultural categories:
  *Festivals, Dance, Music, Handicrafts, Food, Monuments, Folk Traditions, Languages, Clothing, Indigenous Knowledge*.
- Real-time search bar + category pill filters + regional dropdown.
- Interactive modal with deep-dive history, community stewards, audio, and bookmark toggle.

### 8. Student Dashboard
- Learner profile: Name, institution, cultural rank (*Level 4: Cultural Connoisseur*), streak counter (*7 Days*), and XP progress bar.
- Daily Cultural Quests with instant XP claims.
- Bookmarks portfolio syncing items saved from the Discovery Catalog and Living Map.
- Submissions tracking showing status (*Verified* or *Pending Review*).
- **SIH 2026 Certificate Generator**: Printable and downloadable credential with official Government of India / SIH 2026 seal.

### 9. Admin / Content Verification Portal
- Curator dashboard for Ministry of Culture and academic evaluators.
- Overview analytics: Total Submissions, Pending Queue, Published Records, Active Scholars.
- Submissions verification table with interactive review drawer to validate oral audio recordings, photos, and stories.
- Approve / Request Edits workflow with instant status updates.
- **SIH 2026 Demo Controls**: Seed sample data and test +500 XP booster for live judging presentations.

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

### Prerequisites
- Node.js (v18+)

### Quick Start
1. Open a terminal in this directory:
   ```bash
   cd C:\Users\rajav\.gemini\antigravity\scratch\heritage-360
   ```
2. Start the local server:
   ```bash
   node server.js
   ```
   *(or `npm start`)*
3. Open your browser at:
   ```
   http://localhost:3000
   ```
