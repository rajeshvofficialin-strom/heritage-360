Tamil Nadu · Dravidian Heritage ✓ Verified Source

Tamil Nadu

A timeless cradle of Sangam literature, granitic Chola temple spires, classical Bharatanatyam, and unbroken hereditary artisan lineages.

📜Traditions & Lore
Sangam poetry assemblies, Kudavolai democratic village voting, Kolam sacred geometry.
🎉Festivals
Pongal Harvest, Natyanjali Dance Festival, Madurai Chithirai Thiruvizha.
🧵Master Crafts
Kanjeevaram Silk (GI), Swamimalai Bronze Idols (GI), Thanjavur Paintings (GI).
🍲Cuisine
Chettinad Pepper Spices, Madurai Jigarthanda, Filter Coffee, Idli & Sambar.
🪕Music & Dance
Carnatic Classical Music, Bharatanatyam, Nadaswaram & Thavil temple orchestra.
🗣️Languages
Classical Tamil (Sangam roots, 2,500+ years antiquity), Badaga, Irula.
📚 Reference: ASI Epigraphia Indica & IGNCA Southern Regional Archive
